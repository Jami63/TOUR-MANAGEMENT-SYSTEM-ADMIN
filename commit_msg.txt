Add Traveler Directory API endpoint returning all enrolled tour members

Once upon a time, the mobile app had to visit every tour one by one to find out who was traveling.
Now, the backend has a special new doorway (GET /api/travelers) that introduces all travelers at once!

Here is the story of this improvement:
- Created the new /api/travelers endpoint inside TourMemberController.
- It finds every traveler across all tours, whether they have a registered account or are visiting as guest friends.
- It calculates each traveler's fair share of expenses, total payments, and settled badges cleanly.
- Added a full automated test in TourManagementFeaturesTest to make sure all guest friends and managers always show up in the directory.
- All 23 tests pass with shining green checkmarks!
