Travel Together. Plan Smarter. Split Fairly.

Planning a vacation, road trip, or weekend getaway with friends, family, or colleagues? SafarFly is your ultimate all-in-one group travel organizer, expense splitter, and tour manager. Say goodbye to messy spreadsheets, confusing calculations, and awkward money conversations after a trip!

With SafarFly, everyone in the group stays on the same page from departure to final destination. Track collective expenses, record payments, calculate equal shares automatically, and pack like a pro with our smart collaborative backpack checklist.

==================================
KEY FEATURES YOU WILL LOVE:
==================================

✈️ COMPLETE TOUR PLANNING & TIMELINE
• Create detailed multi-day itineraries with multiple destination stops.
• Add trip participants by phone number, email, or direct invite.
• Real-time trip status badges: Live Now, Upcoming, and Completed.
• Clear roles: Assign Tour Managers to oversee operations while members enjoy full transparency.

💰 REAL-TIME SHARED EXPENSE TRACKING
• Record tour expenses instantly on the go (Transport, Food, Hotel, Sightseeing, Fuel, Emergency, and more).
• Upload receipts and photos for total accountability.
• Instant mathematical balance tracking: see how much money has been spent and what remains of your tour budget.

🤝 AUTOMATIC SPLITS & FAIR SETTLEMENTS
• Advanced automated ledger calculates exactly who paid, who owes, and who is owed.
• Strict mathematical fairness: Net Balance = Total Paid − Equal Share of Expenses.
• Instant automated refund calculations when trips wrap up under budget.
• No hidden fees or confusing jargon: 100% transparent math anyone can understand.

🎒 COLLABORATIVE PACKING BACKPACK

• Encrypted data communication keeping your group financials completely private.

⚡ DESIGNED FOR REAL TRAVELERS
• Works smoothly even on spotty mobile data connections.
